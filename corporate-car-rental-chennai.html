Employee Transport in Chennai

Move your people, not your paperwork

Employee transport is one of those services that only gets noticed when it goes wrong — a cab that doesn't show, a driver who doesn't know the route, an invoice nobody can reconcile. S V CABS has spent more than two decades making sure it simply works. Our corporate car rental and employee transport service in Chennai is built for HR, admin and facilities teams who need dependable daily logistics and a single, predictable bill at the end of the month.

We handle the full spread of corporate needs: daily pickup and drop for staff, shift and shuttle services for round-the-clock operations, dedicated cars for management and visiting clients, airport transfers for travelling employees and guests, and one-off logistics for town halls, hiring drives and offsites. Contracts are shaped around your actual operation — your office locations, headcount, shift timings and duty-of-care policy — rather than forced into a fixed package. Rates are agreed upfront and billed monthly on a consolidated invoice, so your finance team gets one clean statement instead of a drawer full of receipts.

Safety and reliability sit at the centre of everything. Our drivers are background-verified, experienced, and briefed on professional conduct and safe-drop protocols for late shifts — because your employees' comfort and security is your reputation too. And because we run an asset-light aggregation model with a wide network of vehicles, we can scale up for a conference or a peak hiring month and scale back down afterwards, so you never pay for idle capacity. From a handful of daily cars to a large managed fleet, we grow with you.

Good to know

Corporate transport FAQs

What corporate transport services does S V CABS provide in Chennai?
We provide daily employee pickup and drop, shift and shuttle services, client and management car rental, airport transfers for staff and visitors, and event or conference logistics. Contracts are tailored to your headcount, locations and shift timings across Chennai.
Do you offer monthly billing and contracts for companies?
Yes. We work on monthly billing with consolidated invoices and clear, agreed rates, so your admin and finance teams have one predictable statement instead of dozens of individual receipts. Contract terms, vehicle counts and routes are set up to match how your company operates.
Are your drivers suitable for employee and women's safety requirements?
Yes. Our drivers are background-verified, experienced and briefed on professional conduct. We understand corporate duty-of-care expectations, including safe drop protocols for late shifts, and we work with your transport policy to keep employees safe and comfortable.
Can you scale up vehicles during peak periods or events?
Yes. Because we operate an asset-light aggregation model with a wide network of vehicles, we can scale from a handful of cars to a large fleet for hiring drives, town halls, offsites and conferences, then scale back down — without you carrying idle capacity.
How do we start a corporate transport contract with S V CABS?
Send us a message on WhatsApp or email with your locations, approximate headcount and shift timings, and our team will prepare a proposal. We have supported Chennai businesses with employee logistics for over two decades and can tailor a plan to your needs.
