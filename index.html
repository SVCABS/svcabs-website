Our Story

Chennai's Most Trusted Name in Transportation

Founded in 2000 in Adyar, S V CABS has grown from a small fleet into Chennai's go-to transportation partner. We serve individuals, corporations, and event organizers — all with the same commitment to safety, punctuality, and comfort.

Our diverse fleet and experienced, verified drivers cater to local city rides, outstation journeys, airport transfers, and large-scale corporate logistics. As we continue to grow, our core values stay unchanged.
