Cab on Hire in Chennai

One car, one driver, for as long as you need them

Some days don't fit into a single point-to-point ride. You have three shops to visit, a couple of meetings across town, a doctor's appointment with a wait in between, or relatives to show around the city. Booking and unbooking separate cabs for each leg is a hassle, and standing in the sun for a new ride at every stop is worse. Hourly car rental in Chennai from S V CABS solves it: you hire a car and driver for a block of time, and the cab stays with you — waiting while you're inside, then driving you to the next stop.

Every hourly rental is chauffeur-driven, so this is a disposal service, not a self-drive rental — you sit back while an experienced driver deals with the traffic, the parking and the routing. Packages are built around hours and kilometres, and we confirm the details and a clear fare on WhatsApp before you begin, so you know exactly what you're paying for. Need a sedan for a solo errand run, an SUV for the family, a tempo traveller for a group, or a luxury car for a special outing? Just tell us your plan and passenger count and we'll suggest the right vehicle and package.

It's a genuinely flexible way to get around. If your plans run long, you can extend on the day and the extra time is simply added at the agreed rate — no scramble to wrap up, no paying for hours you didn't use. And because it's the same trusted S V CABS service we've offered across Chennai since 2000, you get a clean car, a courteous driver and a fair, upfront price every time. For anyone with a busy, multi-stop day ahead, an hourly cab is the calmest way to do it.

Hourly car rental FAQs

How does hourly car rental in Chennai work?
You hire a car and driver for a block of time and use it as you like within the city — the cab stays at your disposal, waiting while you shop, meet or run errands, and taking you between stops. Packages are based on hours and kilometres, and we confirm the details and fare on WhatsApp before you start.
Is the driver included in the hourly rental?
Yes. Every hourly rental comes with an experienced driver. This is a chauffeur-driven disposal service, not a self-drive rental, so you can relax between stops while the driver handles the traffic, parking and routing.
What is an hourly cab best used for?
It is ideal for anything with multiple stops or waiting in between — a day of shopping, back-to-back business meetings, medical appointments, house-hunting, temple visits or showing guests around the city. Instead of booking separate rides, you keep one car and driver for the whole outing.
What happens if I need the car for longer than booked?
That is not a problem. If your plans run over, you can simply extend on the day and the additional time is added at the agreed rate. We keep it flexible so you are never rushing to finish or paying for hours you did not use.
Which vehicles are available for hourly rental?
You can choose a sedan for solo trips and couples, an SUV for families or extra comfort, a tempo traveller for groups, or a luxury car for premium outings. Tell us your plan and passenger count on WhatsApp and we will recommend the right vehicle and package.
