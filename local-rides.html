City Taxi in Chennai

The everyday cab that just turns up

Most trips in a city are small, ordinary and important: getting to work on time, reaching an appointment without stress, dropping a parent at the station, doing the weekend shopping run. A good local cab in Chennai is one you don't have to think about — it arrives when it says it will, the driver knows the way, and the fare is what you were told. That is the service S V CABS has provided across Chennai since 2000, and it is why so many riders keep our number saved.

Booking is deliberately simple. There is no app to download, no account to create and no wallet to top up — you message us on WhatsApp with your pickup and drop, and we confirm a car and a fixed fare in minutes. Pay after the ride, at exactly the price we agreed. Need the cab for a couple of hours to run several errands, or for a full day of meetings across town? Keep the same car and driver on an hourly package and we'll suggest the most economical option for your plan.

Our drivers know the city the way locals do — the shortcuts around Adyar and Mylapore, the flow of OMR and Anna Salai at rush hour, the quiet back-routes through T. Nagar. They drive at a sensible pace, keep the car clean and air-conditioned, and treat regulars and first-timers with the same courtesy. Whether you want a quick point-to-point hop or a dependable ride you can rely on every single day, we cover the whole city, round the clock, at a price you can trust.

Good to know

Local cab FAQs

Can I book a local cab in Chennai for just a short trip?
Yes. We handle short point-to-point local rides across Chennai just as happily as longer ones — a run to the doctor, the railway station, a mall or a meeting. Send your pickup and drop on WhatsApp and we confirm a car and a fixed fare in minutes.
Do you offer local cabs for a full day within the city?
Yes. If you have errands, meetings or shopping across several stops, you can keep the same car and driver for a few hours or the whole day on an hourly package. Tell us roughly how long you need and we will suggest the most economical option.
Are your local drivers familiar with Chennai's roads?
Yes. Our drivers are experienced with Chennai's neighbourhoods and traffic, from Adyar and T. Nagar to OMR, Anna Nagar and Velachery. They take sensible routes and know how to work around peak-hour congestion so you reach on time.
How do I pay for a local ride?
You pay after the ride, at the fixed fare we confirm when you book. There is no advance payment and no app wallet to top up — the price we agree on WhatsApp is what you settle at the end of the trip.
Which areas of Chennai do you cover for local rides?
We cover the whole city, including Adyar, Besant Nagar, T. Nagar, Velachery, OMR, Anna Nagar, Porur, Guindy, Nungambakkam, Mylapore, Tambaram and everywhere in between. If you are within Chennai, we can pick you up.
