Outstation Travel from Chennai

Leave Chennai the comfortable way

A long drive should be the easy part of your trip, not the stressful one. Whether you are heading to Pondicherry for the weekend, making the pilgrimage to Tirupati, or travelling to Bangalore for work, an outstation cab from Chennai gives you your own car and driver for the whole journey — no crowded buses, no rigid train timings, and no negotiating a fresh fare at every stop. S V CABS has run outstation trips out of Chennai since 2000, and we have built the service around comfort, safety and a price you can trust.

You choose how you travel. A one-way cab is perfect when you are not coming back the same day — you pay only for the single leg and we handle the rest. A round trip keeps the same car and driver with you, which is usually the better value for same-day returns and short getaways. Tell us your plan on WhatsApp and we will recommend whichever option is cheaper for your route, then confirm a fixed fare that covers the driver and the kilometres. Tolls, parking and state permits are billed as actuals and explained upfront, so the number you see at the end is the number you expected.

Every outstation vehicle is cleaned and serviced before it leaves, and every driver knows the highways — the ECR and OMR coastal routes to Pondicherry, the NH48 run to Bangalore, the ghats up to Tirupati — so you can relax, stop for a meal when you like, and reach rested. For families and groups, our SUVs and tempo travellers give you the room for luggage and company; for solo trips and couples, a sedan keeps it economical. Whatever the destination, the promise is the same: a safe, unhurried journey at an honest, agreed price.

Good to know

Outstation cab FAQs

What is the difference between a one-way and round-trip outstation cab?
A one-way outstation cab drops you at your destination and you pay only for that single journey, which is ideal when you are not returning the same day. A round trip keeps the same car and driver with you for the return leg, which usually works out cheaper per kilometre for same-day or short trips. Tell us your plan and we recommend the cheaper option for you.
Are your outstation fares fixed, and what do they include?
Yes. We quote a fixed fare on WhatsApp before you travel, based on your destination, vehicle and trip type. The quote covers the driver and the kilometres for your route. Toll, parking, permit and any state entry charges are billed as actuals, and we tell you clearly upfront so there are no surprises at the end.
Which outstation destinations do you cover from Chennai?
We run cabs from Chennai to popular destinations including Pondicherry, Tirupati, Bangalore, Vellore, Mahabalipuram, Kanchipuram, Yelagiri, Chidambaram and many more across Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka. If your destination is not listed, send it to us on WhatsApp and we will quote it.
Can I book an outstation cab for a multi-day trip?
Yes. For temple tours, weekend getaways and multi-city itineraries we provide the car and driver for the full duration. Multi-day trips include a nominal driver allowance for overnight stays, which we explain when we send your quote so the total is clear before you commit.
How early should I book an outstation cab from Chennai?
For weekday trips a few hours' notice is usually enough, but for weekends, long weekends and festival dates we strongly recommend booking a day or two ahead to secure your preferred vehicle. You can reserve any time on WhatsApp — we operate 24 hours a day.
